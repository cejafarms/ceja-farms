Complete Vineyard Solutions

Whether you’re removing an old vineyard, developing new acreage or bringing in a harvest, Ceja Farms delivers reliable service and proven results.

Vineyard Removal

Complete removal and disposal of existing vineyards. We handle everything from pulling posts and wires to grinding and hauling debris.

Vineyard Planting & Development

Site preparation, staking, planting and vineyard establishment for new and replanted acreage. Built for long-term success.

Grape Harvesting

Dependable harvest crews and equipment. We work efficiently to bring in a clean crop at the right time.

Vineyard Maintenance Support

Additional field services to keep your vineyard productive including trellis repair, re-staking, re-plant and more.

On the Job

Removal, start to finish.
